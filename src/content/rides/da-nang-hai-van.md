Two days in central Vietnam, riding from Da Nang to the Hai Van Pass.

The first afternoon was Son Tra: the Lady Buddha above the trees, the courtyard and the hall,
then the coast road with the sea on one side and the green hill on the other. In the evening,
My Khe — the beach still busy, the city lit up behind it.

The next morning was the ride north: the bay, the sign on the sand, and then the pass itself —
hairpins, goats, a wooden shack, and a chair with a view. The top was all cloud and blue water,
and the road down to the north.
