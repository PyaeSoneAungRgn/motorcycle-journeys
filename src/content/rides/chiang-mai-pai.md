No loop this time — just the places. A New Year week north of Chiang Mai, with the bike pointed
at whatever looked good on the map and the evenings left open.

The first morning was coffee at The First Valley, high above Mae Rim with the valley still under
cloud. Two days later, Khun Chang Khian: wild Himalayan cherry in bloom, a wooden viewpoint and a
sea of mist below it. Then the long road to Pai for the turn of the year — the canyon, the Chinese
village at Yun Lai, khao soi, and a sunset that took its time.

The last day was Chiang Dao, mountains and cloud and a viewpoint that made the whole week feel
like a detour worth taking. Seven days, one small bike, and no straight line back.
