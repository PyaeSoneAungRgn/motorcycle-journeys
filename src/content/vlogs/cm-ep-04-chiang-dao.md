The last day of the trip was a day ride: north from Chiang Mai to Chiang Dao, then west into the
mountains where the road climbs above the cloud. The weather did most of the work — sun breaking
through, then nothing but grey, then sun again.

We sat on the deck for a while and let it change. Then the long road back to Chiang Mai, and the
end of the week.
