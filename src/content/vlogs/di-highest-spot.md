The ride starts in Chiang Mai before light and climbs to the highest point in Thailand. The cloud
sea was still sitting in the valley when we stopped, and the summit was cold and quiet apart from
the shrine and the sign.

The way down took longer than the way up: the royal pagodas, the flower gardens and the fog.
