The first long-form episode from Vietnam, filmed over two days in September 2025. The ride
starts at the Dragon Bridge, loops around Son Tra for the Lady Buddha and the coast road, then
heads north along the bay and up the Hai Van Pass.

Most of the episode is the road and the weather — the city behind, the sea below, and the pass
doing its slow reveal.
