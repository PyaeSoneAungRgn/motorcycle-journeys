The second film is the temple and the way home. The Daibutsu above the trees, fourteen metres of
green copper; the kitsune lined up along the wall with their red bibs; the golden gate with the
valley behind it.

Then the superhighway again, north this time, with the light going and the hill dropping away
behind the trees.

Two parts, one day, and a statue that does not look like it belongs in Lampang.
