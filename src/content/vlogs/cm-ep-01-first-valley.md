The first ride of the week: out of Chiang Mai early, north through Mae Rim and into the hills
before the day warmed up. The First Valley was already busy with people who had the same idea.

Most of the episode is the room and the view — the coffee bar, the fire, and the valley doing
its slow morning thing below the terrace.
