The road to Pai is the reason people come here: more curves than straight, a long climb through
forest, and the valley appearing all at once at the end. We stopped at a cafe south of town,
walked the red ridge of the canyon, and found a field full of people for the last sunset of the
year.

The episode ends with the sun going down behind the ridge — the same thirty seconds that became
the time lapse on the ride page, only slower.
