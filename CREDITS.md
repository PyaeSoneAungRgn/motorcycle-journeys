# Credits

## Photography

All photographs on this site are © **Pyae Sone Aung, 2024–2026**, taken during the Nan Loop in
Nan province (13–17 October 2026), the Chiang Mai & Pai trip in northern Thailand
(28 December 2025 – 3 January 2026), the Da Nang & Hai Van Pass ride in central Vietnam
(20–21 September 2025), the Doi Inthanon day ride in Chiang Mai province (10 May 2025) and the
Daibutsu day ride to Doi Phra Chan in Lampang province (14 December 2024). All rights reserved.
They are not licensed for reuse without permission.

The images in `src/assets/photos/` were resized to 1600px on the long edge and stripped of metadata
for the web. Capture dates and coordinates in `src/data/photos.json` come from the originals' EXIF.

## Films

The eleven short films and three time lapses across the Nan Loop, Chiang Mai & Pai, Da Nang & Hai
Van Pass and Doi Inthanon pages are © **Pyae Sone Aung, 2025–2026**. They are self-hosted H.264
MP4 files in `public/videos/`, trimmed for the web, and all rights are reserved.

The nine long-form episodes — five from Chiang Mai & Pai (including the Pai sunset short), two
from the Daibutsu day ride, one from Da Nang to the Hai Van Pass and one from Doi Inthanon — are
published on YouTube and embedded on this site through click-to-load `youtube-nocookie.com`
players, so no YouTube cookies are set until a visitor presses play. All rights in those videos
remain with their creators.

The 13 one-off films on **Other places** — day rides around Chiang Mai from Mon Jam and Samoeng
Forest to Mae Kampong, Doi Pui, Doi Kham and Tha Phae Gate — are also published on YouTube and
linked from their place cards. The cards use local 16:9 posters made from the channel's own
thumbnails; all rights remain with the creator.
